How should the predictors be measured?

Enter the study-coded values, not substituted scales. Cognition is CHARLS word recall (0–20) divided by 20; depressive symptoms are CESD-10 (0–30) divided by 30. Mobility and activity use harmonized survey definitions. See the full predictor dictionary for coding and cross-cohort differences.

What does functional impairment mean here?

New impairment in any of six ADL domains (bathing, dressing, eating, transferring, toileting, continence) or in shopping/cooking IADL at follow-up. Participants were unimpaired at baseline, aged ≥60 in development and ≥65 in external validation, and had at least two of seven harmonized chronic conditions.

How is the estimate calculated?

The page averages probabilities from 20 frozen StandardScaler–XGBoost pipelines trained on the study’s multiply imputed development datasets. All 12 inputs are required, so no new-input imputation is performed. Models, preprocessing and the threshold have not been retrained or reoptimized. This edition provides the probability and binary classification; it does not provide individual SHAP explanations.

What are the important limitations?

Follow-up windows and cognition measures differ across cohorts. Activity was substantially missing in development. Subgroup fairness and the impact of using the tool to guide care have not been established. Age above 95 or a count of 7 conditions extends beyond the observed development range. External calibration slope was 1.414, calibration-in-the-large 0.006, and Brier score 0.186.

Can I use this offline, and where do my inputs go?

Download the complete repository folder and open index.html. The calculator uses bundled scripts and model parameters, without a prediction API, analytics or external fonts. Inputs remain in the current page’s memory unless you download a result. When used on GitHub Pages, the hosting provider may record ordinary website access information.
